This review inspected actual repository source and existing evidence; no independent runtime, hardware benchmark or image/capture inspection was performed. The current environment-only plan excludes all colonist/actor/navigation workloads and prioritizes reusable development and continuous terrain LOD.

Assessment

M1 core aged editable terrain and fresh-world admission are implemented in bounded cases. M2 and M3 have working upstream-derived physical kernels and a shared accepted clock, but normal-world coverage is still pilot-limited. M4 has real clouds and bounded flow-driven cuts, not general deposition or lateral migration/cutoffs. M5 has 64/128/256/512km save/reopens and 4,000km macro opening with two water neighborhoods (no regional weather or actors), not a representative full environment load. The latest 512km rendered day achieved 600s in 75.043s, zero ending debt, p95/p99 16.157/24.747ms; 32x reopen p95/p99 20.215/24.986ms, missed frame target. Sixteen actors, 25 atmosphere columns and one open pair. Preserve the user-confirmed 1:1 physical clock; 8x baseline, 32x stretch. Do not chase stretch performance before regional functionality.

Source-confirmed coverage bottleneck

  • Normal initialization fills marine stocks then selects the first eligible nonmarine connection and returns after initializing its coupled weather. Surface preparation creates a broader finite graph but ordinary links begin closed; formation basin capacity is not itself water. Natural connected links must operate in the normal world, not just one commanded demonstration. Initialize plausible finite stock and catchment feeding/spin-up; preserve closed controls and unresolved contacts.
  • LandWater has exactly two columns and two aquifers. Weather construction crops around those two receivers, uses terrain sample spacing, caps columns at 4096, and maps only those land/reach footprints. The warm regional 100x100km evidence used 2km² connected land (0.02% of domain area); approximately 354,410m³ of rain outside footprints stayed in weather ground stock, whereas about 1,677.57m³ entered CFE and 4.54m³ entered reaches directly. Rain exchange retains uncovered ground water and explicitly never reoffers it. This is finite accounting, not whole-area runoff. Generalize to all represented physical surfaces with conservative coarse catchments, no need for per-metre land owners. Route existing pending/retained stocks coherently; don’t silently discard rain. CFE interface has fixed 1,800 physical-second interval (three default 600s game days at 1:1); assess meaningful storm response and donor-consistent configurable cadence, not arbitrary coefficient/timestep changes.
  • Surface solver scans all stores/links per substep and returns before accepting high-Froude flow. Surface owner supports one 3x3 floodplain and singleton watched/delegated state. Regional wetting and rapid transfer must use independent connected storage, sparse actual work and bounded local detail. A steep coast or rapid patch must not permanently stop unrelated simulation; preserve finite custody and coherent time. Prior edited fine return lacks coarse survey coverage. Reuse proven kernels; generalize owning records and boundaries, don’t multiply copies of isolated pilots or create a second production engine.
  • Normal repair refuses survey counts other than one. Normal water view creates two bounded 240m strips, with 512m visibility distance, and hides on nonzero world revision. Generalize current-geometry repair and meaningful depth/velocity/hazard queries, show actual visible rivers, lakes, coast/flood water from current geometry, and update dirty portions rather than globally hiding. Normal gameplay edits and hazards are not yet accepted. No full navigation or ECS rewrite in V1.
  • Cold exchange applies current warm liquid exchange only with both air and water >=273.15K. Mixed regional receivers had air about 262–266K and zero CFE potential ET; snow/rain/melt still functioned. Zero potential ET in cold saturated conditions is not itself invalid. Do not force positive ET or choose an easier test to hide missing behavior. Implement physically eligible cold-air/open-liquid sensible and latent exchange with finite temperature/phase limits and appropriate existing bulk methods, and distinguish frozen surface vs unfrozen water. Retain warm case. The observed domain peak wind 77.787m/s warrants a bounded plausibility diagnosis if persistent, not a new circulation search.

Recommended next implementation packages

  1. Generalize current V1 physical coverage first: persistent natural water connections, coarse land/groundwater owners, real full-area precipitation allocation and independent coarse weather grid; map by area/geometry and use physical activity, not camera. Preserve finite pending amounts, stable IDs, common clock, snapshots and new V1 persistence; no legacy migration requirement. Progress from a complete catchment to neighboring catchments, not another selected pair.
  2. Use this coverage to complete actual above-bank regional flooding and recession, connected lake/ocean/cave/spring contacts, automatic local rapid custody/return, and cold surface exchange. Reuse Wflow/FluxCell/CFE/Weather3D/CliMA/COARE donors, no broad solver replacement or additional standalone campaign. Fix actual unsupported contacts, not artificial gate success.
  3. Generalize current-geometry edits, derived queries and nav hazard publication; render all relevant visible water including repaired channels/basins, continue actual sediment/erosion and MeanderPy bank migration/deposition and cutoffs. Keep visual detail independent of physical ownership. Complete coherent normal gameplay.
  4. Complete the existing M5 representative combined 100km severe storm/recession, rendered 512km at 8x with meaningful simultaneous coverage/actors, and 4,000km macro plus active regions; measure inclusive work, frame/debt/memory and actual counts. Avoid premature 32x tuning, repeated old regressions and repeated identical tiny smoke trials. Record exact status and failures. Continue all authorized milestones without new user approval for routine engineering. Escalate only fundamental dependency/requirement blockers.

See technical plan, execution protocol, progress, and M1, M2, M3 evidence. Worker/orchestrator remain owners of active progress and implementation. No changed runtime, new solver, clock, scope or fixed tuning settings authorized by this review.
