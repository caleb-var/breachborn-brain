Integrated living-world foundation selection

Research date: 25 November 2026. This records a design preference and qualification plan, not runtime authorization, implementation status or passed validation. Source review covered the canonical private repository and the generated public Brain mirror. The public mirror is not an authored source. No game code changes or solver benchmarks were performed. The user explicitly soft-locked GeoGenesis plus selected PWG algorithms and retained Zylann; they favor HPA/DHPA and asked to close remaining selections through compatibility research. Other choices below are proposed first candidates, not user approval or production acceptance.

Source-screen findings (not benchmarks)

  • Wflow.jl, MIT, pin c617a12e413e5eaf20b1818a4091f552001910c7: staggered surface routing, floodplain storage, reservoir and groundwater implementations. Wflow FAQ documents instantaneous exchange and potential excessive inundation. Its negative-storage corrections must not become unaccounted mass losses. Use as narrow donor/reference, not whole Julia runtime. SFINCS (GPL-3.0) is mathematical subgrid reference, not a code-embedding choice; Clawpack Riemann (BSD-3-Clause), pin 008d4921ea46878a97a96c8b8daa399a2e7b9078, is exceptional-flow candidate only. Landlab remains independent reference, not a default solver with unaccounted thin film.
  • Canonical source snapshot c7b4ede9af45a67a6ddd3d65cd5685d3af79c320 includes native hydraulic flux and finite exchange, groundwater head/conductance, conservative face vapor transport, incision and geology deposit operations. This is evidence of available calculations, not validated integrated performance. Reuse isolated operations and known-answer tests, not the rejected legacy graph/preparation architecture. CFE source pin f9182dfb3d81c407c66c5b782114173893c30cf2; requalify adapter/cadence/conservation.
  • Weather3D MIT, pin ce66920fd9515e3842110b9c2c7340658e289bef: pe.wgsl coarse hydrostatic anelastic C-grid circulation, vertical mass continuity, pressure projection and column physics. Earth-specific channel/boundary assumptions, advection and GPU dependencies require redesign/qualification; no claim of conservative transport or validated performance for Breachborn. Author describes early AI-assisted project; audit source provenance and third-party notices. Full sim.wgsl is not selected. WeatherBox MIT pin 7d6b2d1e5fcde3ea7a365e398664827563a939ce is a 2D thermodynamic/dynamics test reference, not conservative full-world transport. SpeedyWeather EUPL-1.2, VolcanicSkies GPL-2 and scientific models are reference-only under present licensing/runtime preferences.

Evaporation tendency explicitly requires timestep limits. Bulk microphysics API documents one-moment rain/snow/melting/sublimation alternatives. ERF pin e215059ffd648ea097f823cfac1869c16e725669, BSD-style license, Kessler scalar warm-rain routines, AMReX wrappers not selected. Audit any transitive source before reuse.
  • NOAA COARE, MIT pin bd1cac80d2dae454e699f9ef46204dfd88086a71: Fortran COARE3.6 module has wind stress/sensible/latent flux outputs. Reference for bulk approximation; do not assume full iterative calculation is cheap enough everywhere.
  • Harris and Lastra, Real-Time Cloud Rendering: precomputed light and impostor approach.   • MOLASSES GPL-3.0 reference only, does not model actual rate/dynamics; MrLavaLoba2 Apache-2.0 inspected license, stochastic elliptical lobe footprint, not a time-dependent lava solver. First lava law remains an independently qualified small kernel.
  • Recast/Detour, zlib, pin 9f4ce64458dfae86e1239c525ddc219c4e9e06f1. Local tiled navigation and links, not a continent-scale hierarchy. Flecs external tasks and enkiTS support scheduler integration; measure rather than adopt a second pool automatically. Pin all new dependencies and their notices before code adoption.

Compatibility and qualification

Use one spatial addressing/feature identity convention, not a common resolution. Illustrative 4,000 km world: 4 km macro geography; 16 km page directory; 1 km/250 m conditional geographic refinement; 16–32 km atmosphere horizontally, 6–12 vertical layers, 4–8 km regional weather trial. Exact scales are candidates. At eight layers a 32 km square-grid atmosphere contains 125,000 cells, about 3.81 MiB at hypothetical 32 B/cell; 16 km gives 500,000 cells / 15.26 MiB; 8 km gives 2 million / 61.04 MiB. Excludes faces, snapshots, solvers, geometry, scratch and all other systems. Nested regions must replace or partition parent mass, not duplicate it.

Shared geometry describes material, occupancy, bed/crest, stage-volume, connectivity, exposure, passability and feature revisions. Hydraulic graph, atmospheric cells, navigation graph and voxel pages use their own resolution and ownership. Each physical inventory has exactly one owner. Finite rain/ET/recharge transfers commit once; accumulate physical elapsed time across differing update intervals. Use conservative surface overlap mapping, explicit incoming/outgoing amounts and energy balances. Neither rendering nor paging owns physical state.

Proposed qualification order:

  1. Narrow independent known-answer tests for formation/refinement, hydraulic and atmospheric laws; freeze tolerances and realistic workloads first. Compare source references and cheap alternatives. No unbounded rewrite.
  2. A connected small world fixture with hill/rain shadow, small creeks, lake/outlet, protective wall/dam, underground compartment, doors/bridge, soil/aquifer, actual water/vapor and heat cycle, plus save/reload and out-of-view edits. Verify conservation, meaningful travel/arrival times, topology, erosion and visuals. Cloud silhouette and presentation tests with actual weather.
  3. Scale the macro addressing and generation, sparse residency, large storm and navigational long route before production integration. Include 100 x 100 km severe storm; 256 km generation baseline and 4,000 km macro; 800 km route and realistic mixed 1k/10k/100k population workloads. Monitor CPU/game-second, GPU/frame, RAM/VRAM, main-thread handoff, latency, debt, saves and work budgets. Preserve prior targets as evaluation guidance rather than assert passed: reference Ryzen 7 3700X, RTX 2070 SUPER, 32 GB; 25 CPU-ms/game-second historical environment target, 8x rendered goal and 32–64x longer-term aspiration. Explicit 600 s/day, 60 days/year calendar and physical-domain rates; no hidden accelerated-time conversion.
  4. Only after passing the bounded, connected fixture and scale gates, expand environmental integration and colony/ecology. Profile inclusive cost, not isolated empty-kernel benchmarks.

Open risks: regional hydrological topology and geometry construction; conservative reduced atmosphere and wind closure; coupled hydraulic stability and editable barriers; cave volumes; cold precipitation/heat closure; cloud rendering softness/performance; actual large-population throughput. Retain open question records until reproducible evidence. The selections are compatible by design contract, not yet validated together. No runtime work authorized by this documentation change.

Review correction: 25 November 2026

The user explicitly rejected treating this preliminary review as a satisfactory technology lock. Existing native environment calculations have not earned a reuse preference. The previously recorded terrain soft lock stands as a user preference, not validation. Reopen water, atmosphere and cloud implementation selections. Do not treat the table above as an authorized implementation plan. User explicitly requires true volumetric, attractive clouds with distinct realistic-stylized shapes; cheap raymarching is acceptable. Geometry and sprite-only clouds are not the primary target. See independent numerical probes and revised shortlist.

The revised hypothesis is a single conservative water inventory with cheap reach routing in proven ordinary downstream segments, connected subgrid hydraulics for low-Froude backwater/floodplain flow, and full shallow-water momentum transport where the simplified law fails. FluxCell is the leading detailed-flow evaluation candidate, not a selected port. For atmospheric transport, independently tested MC-limited conservative advection outperformed basic upwind in shape retention, but narrow storms still require finer/subgrid treatment. Pressure/wind dynamics, vertical transport, surface heat and phase changes are unqualified. Prototype a reduced CPU circulation and compare against a more complete reference before choosing it. True low-resolution, depth-aware volumetric clouds using reusable bounded density shapes, empty-space skipping and temporal reconstruction are preferred for visual testing. SunshineClouds2 and Godot-Cloud-Worlds are source-screened donors, not performance-accepted.

Complementary pressure review

Integrated pressure review reconciles the latest user correction, sibling dam-break/cloud evidence and complementary cost/clock, topology and transport tests. Adds PyFlwDir geometry, mizuRoute IRF, Wflow subgrid/snow, CliMT/0M/COARE and Clawpack/JCM leads. No preference for existing native mechanisms, no full-stack validation, no new technology lock. Independent pressure-test update (25 November 2026)

See executed architecture pressure tests. The architecture is not qualified for production. The tests strengthen shared finite ownership, feature-aware hydraulic geometry, HPA-style multi-layer portals and bounded true volumetric raymarching as directions, but falsify several shortcuts: average elevations, single-reservoir/pure-delay river routing, naive subgrid stability timestep, blanket fine flooding and global 8km atmospheric grid at historical alpha144, fixed cloud lighting. A synthetic 500-step hydraulic comparison diverged by up to 2.10m at the original timestep despite conserved mass; half timestep agreement is not physical acceptance. The actual environmental clock and stability policy are first-order architecture decisions. Retain terrain soft lock only as user-stated preference; no upstream formation/solver, target GPU, integrated simulation or population scale test was executed. Do not promote earlier preferred numerical implementation entries to accepted status. Use the bounded qualification sequence in the evidence record, with actual pinned solver and Godot tests.

Replacement clarification (26 November 2026)

The user explicitly challenged revising the failed water system instead of replacing it with known-good technology. For new worlds, replacement is the objective, consistent with DEC-REUSE. Do not retain the old hydraulic architecture, river preparation, or numerical kernels by default. Standalone experimental kernels in the pressure probes are falsification/reference fixtures, not a proposed new bespoke water implementation. First evaluate actual unmodified upstream numerical implementations and their published validation, then minimally adapt only the selected candidate’s data/geometry/coupling boundaries. Wflow is the first permissive regional river/flood/land-water candidate to execute; SFINCS is a mature independent subgrid reference, not approved for embedding under GPL-3.0; FluxCell is the first detailed shallow-water candidate to execute, not a proven global system. mizuRoute ordinary reach routing is conditional only if the regional engine demonstrably needs cheaper upstream routing. Avoid selecting three simultaneous runtime solvers before evidence establishes the need. Wflow’s instantaneous same-stage river/floodplain exchange and FluxCell’s fixed solid mask are significant integration risks. No external solver is yet validated against the complete mutable 4,000 km game or target hardware. Retain only useful engine integration, geometry/edit interfaces and test evidence, without requiring legacy compatibility. No runtime authorization or final numerical selection follows from this clarification.

26 November: water-first evidence update

See upstream water evidence and Q-FLOOD. User requests continued research until terrain compatibility, scale and gameplay are confirmed. No production lock. ADCIRC v56.2.1 enters regional qualification alongside independent SFINCS reference; Wflow remains a land-water candidate but its stock floodplain storage and clipping are blockers for universal use. No new code, numerical port or license commitment authorized.

Stylized water clarification (26 November 2026)

User prefers fewer meaningful side-streams, curving beautiful realistic-stylized rivers and flow/rainfall-responsive evolution over scientific precision. Re-rank water candidates on cost, physical consistency and gameplay rather than forecast-grade detail; favor testing existing inexpensive 1D reach and connected subgrid storage methods first, with detailed FluxCell only where needed. Keep ADCIRC and SFINCS as regional challengers/references; no new solver lock, Wflow’s shared-stage and negative-clipping issues remain. Generate a sparse physical network from aged geography, aggregate omitted minor runoff conservatively and expose configurable density/shape. Maintain independent geometry/solver sampling, real cross-sections and lengths. For slow morphology investigate MeanderPy with actual accumulated hydraulic forcing; not random visual animation or another whole-world erosion engine. Retain finite mass, connectivity/barriers, meaningful arrival/flooding, persistent edits, atmospheric exchanges and appropriate accuracy. Suggested old precision gates are not user-approved production thresholds; choose gameplay-scaled tolerances before tests, never relax after failures. No target hardware or full integration validation.
