Environmental Foundation V1 — implementation-first plan

Environmental Foundation V1 — implementation-first plan

26 November 2026. The user agreed to the implementation-first direction: choose evidence-backed upstream numerical implementations, stop prolonged standalone comparative campaigns, and create a concrete continuation plan. This is the selected provisional design baseline, not a declaration that an integrated solver or the performance target has passed. It supersedes the research-first sequencing of water-first qualification; preserve that document and pressure-test evidence, upstream water evidence, reuse decision, formation decision, and requirements. No runtime source changed by this document. The user subsequently authorized full replacement implementation in Codex on 26 November 2026; see execution protocol and the current task scope. Actual runtime implementation has not started by virtue of this documentation change.

1. Deliverable and scope

Deliver a normal playable Godot fresh-world foundation with aged realistic-stylized terrain, distinct curved rivers and meaningful tributaries, finite lakes/ocean, land/soil and groundwater, causal rain/evaporation/heat/wind, connected flooding and recession, local interactive water, slow erosion/migration and real attractive volumetric clouds. Include persistent current geometry and edits, cheap native gameplay field queries and navigation-hazard invalidation. Scale by geography and actual work, not camera visibility; a 100×100 km storm, offscreen changes and a 4,000 km macro configuration are required scale evidence, not optional presentation. Full 100k+ population, complete ecology, all liquid families, advanced abilities and final art content are later dependent scopes; retain their contracts, don’t claim them complete or create irreversible limits. Basic groundwater/cave contacts, finite ocean and cold-phase water cycle are required environmental closure cases, not optional infinite sources.

One primary implementation per role. Ordinary small correctness fixtures, source equivalence and profiling occur while delivering features. No parallel full solver implementations, bespoke PDE engine, reimplementation of vendor test catalogs, revival of LandscapeChannels, or reuse preference for failed native environment mechanisms. Existing Godot/Jolt, Zylann, useful terrain editing, coordinates and integration boundaries may be retained. Preserve legacy artifacts and unpublished work; fresh-world policy is not a migration mandate.

2. Dependency and numerical reuse manifest

Pins are previously inspected source snapshots, not necessarily latest releases or a transitive license approval. Preserve notices and independently check selected files/build dependencies before incorporation. Prefer original callable kernels over copying a whole application; native ports must preserve the upstream numerical method. Record the exact upstream files used and deviations. Do not embed unapproved GPL/AGPL code.

3. Final source-level reuse review

Wflow: surface_process.jl takes prior discharge, two stages, face water depth, area, hydraulic radius, path length, squared Manning roughness, Froude limit and dt. Signed semi-implicit friction law is directly separable. Floodplain profile functions provide depth/flow area/wetted perimeter/storage inversion. Groundwater and river-groundwater contact are narrow donors. Its regional storage update merges river and floodplain to common stage and records negative corrections after clipping; do not preserve these semantics. Stable timestep is a depth/length bound for its discretization, not proof for new subgrid graph. Use existing update loops where compatible; independent compartments, connected face geometry, simultaneous finite transfer acceptance, topology repair and subgrid stability are genuine integration changes. A Froude cap cannot be used to suppress a rapid flood. Earlier independent probe shows dry-dam front error about 54% for simplified local inertia. Keep it for ordinary low-Froude flows; promote or use a qualified shock-capable representation when needed.

Weather3D: pe.wgsl computes hydrostatic and vertical mass continuity; momentum and nonconservative scalar transport are in pe_stage. pe.js projects each of three RK stages, with variable-column-mass iterations using direct DFT/inverse transforms and Thomas solve. For square 125²/250² grid, one direct forward/inverse pair per each of three stages is 6*N³ = 11,718,750 / 93,750,000 inner summation terms per full timestep, excluding iterations and column work. Do not port direct DFT literally. Reuse an established permissively licensed fast transform/linear-algebra backend suitable for the chosen boundary conditions (audit existing dependencies first), and retain variable-coefficient correction and residual control where needed. Do not assume periodic-x/closed-y fits a bounded world; choose saved boundary policy and explicit fluxes, e.g. compatible no-normal-flow boundaries with matching transform/solver, or a deliberately periodic world. Reference profiles, Earth-dependent nudging and independent soil/moisture/SST forcing cannot create water/energy; disable or replace with accounted game forcing. Conservative face transport must use the same dry-air mass flux, include vertical face transfers and consistent latent/sensible energy. This is a reduced-model port, not a turnkey atmosphere. Do not present dropping pressure correction or clamping negative humidity as an optimization pass.

4. Shared physical representation

  • Whole-world metric coordinates (checked wide integer identity; local float offsets), independent solver lattices. Initial configurable macro geography ~4km for 4,000km; 16km addressing pages, not solver boundaries; 32km atmosphere with 8 layers as initial cost-conscious starting point, local/nested 16km or smaller where justified. Pilot smaller domain with appropriate resolution, not a single uniform 32km cell presented as a storm.
  • Aged geometry produces persistent feature IDs, channel polylines/splines and true length, bed/bank profiles, cross sections, crest/sill elevations, compartments/outlet connectivity, V(stage), wet area and face conveyance, material/soil/roughness/cover, cave contacts. Fine realization and all derived tables agree with the same recipe+edits. No render-stage-driven recarving, no global metre DEM/SDF scans. Regional fine sampling only where required; keep hydrographic topology correct across page boundaries. Sparse channel density around 0.1 km/km² of land and average 2km hydraulic segments are proposed configurable starting settings, not arbitrary minimum/maximum or user-approved immutable counts. Retain meaningful 1m creeks, unbounded meaningful river widths, natural confluences, beautiful curved planform; omitted minor drainage contributes actual catchment runoff and can produce local wetness.
  • Surface authoritative quantities: volume, stage derived from current geometry, signed flow/momentum as required by selected method, thermal and relevant constituent mass. Soil owns actual storage and runoff queue; spatial groundwater owns aquifer head/storage; ocean is finite. Air owns dry-air convention, temperature/energy, wind/pressure, vapor/condensate/falling precipitation. Geometry has a revision; each field snapshot has coverage/precision/time, not an alternative physical owner. No per-renderer state as physical truth.
  • At a common accepted model interval: air supplies integrated precipitation; partition once by receiving area and geometry; soil yields runoff/recharge; surface accepts flow and withdrawals; actual ET/open-water evaporation/heat return once; record finite pending transfers until consumed. Conservative sums, nonnegative donor amounts, and overlap in space/time. Update material only from accumulated real erosion/deposition; refresh affected geometry/navigation and conserve water when volume changes. Refinement replaces parent authority for represented volume, and coarsening reconciles actual stock/tracer/heat and timing; no double counting. Trigger by physical state or interactions, not camera. Renderer reads snapshots only.

5. Clocks and engineering cost envelope

Retain configurable 600s/day, 60 days/year calendar. Do not require 144 physical seconds per game-second for every physical process simply to match an Earth day. Proposed first playable setting: common water/atmosphere physical clock of 1 physical second per simulated game-second, separately saved calendar/solar forcing. This is an explicit proposed fantasy time-scale choice for responsive interaction, not user-approved final clock. At 8x simulation a 20 physical-second release lasts 2.5 real seconds at alpha1, 0.208s at alpha12, 0.0174s at alpha144. Weather movement, water cycle, runoff queues and season pacing must be coherent under the declared clock; no pretending this is an Earth-time 24h day, no independent camera clock. If pacing is too slow, adjust a declared world/physical policy with measured cost, not silent dt compression. Historical alpha144 remains a sensitivity comparator.

Arithmetic from recorded synthetic EPYC probes and upstream claim, not target performance: 50% land over 16Mkm² ×0.1km/km² /2km =400k reaches; 64B/reach illustrative 24.4MiB before curves, edges, scratch, saves. At 29.8ns/update, one full update is 11.92ms; at hypothetical stable dt300 physical seconds, alpha1/12/144 =0.040/0.477/5.722 aggregate CPU-ms/game-s. At dt60s =0.199/2.384/28.608. This does not establish actual graph or Wflow update cost/stability. A 100x100km storm with 250m cells previously measured synthetic 45.92 CPU-ms/game-s at alpha144 -> .319 at alpha1; 1km .761 ->.0053, not proof of comparable fidelity. 32/16km atmosphere transport+column probes .77/4.95 at alpha144 -> .0053/.0344 at alpha1; dynamics not included. Actual cadence constrained by geometry, dynamics and accuracy. Do not infer combined budget from these estimates. 256² at 1m, dt=.05s, 8x at alpha1 needs 10.49M cell updates/s, nominal .287 GPU-ms/frame at FluxCell’s author-reported 610M/s; at alpha144 41.26ms/frame, before integration. Do not use high-resolution solver everywhere. Budget from actual complete workload and peak service cost, not divided by worker count.

Reference target: Ryzen 7 3700X, RTX2070 SUPER, 32GB, 1080p/60fps, sustained 8x without growing debt (future 32–64x aspiration). No such integrated result is claimed. Historical aggregate environment CPU target 25ms/game-s, 512MiB steady, 768MiB autosave, 1GiB replacement, .5ms water GPU/frame and 32MiB water VRAM are comparison targets, not newly approved rigid subsystem allocations. Broad flood/edits, memory, CPU/GPU/frame, query and publication cost and save debt included. Report peak latency and complete work. Choose coarse connected storage and multirate scheduling consistent with actual transfer clocks, not just arbitrary reduced cadence or fine region caps.

6. One release in six implementation milestones

Proceed to the next milestone when the implemented output is usable and its directly relevant acceptance criteria are met. These are delivery milestones, not a separate exploratory research campaign. Reuse upstream checks and the same one connected world; no endless independent benchmark phase. If a hard failure occurs, record the smallest specific issue, fix the integration when bounded, or stop that component and reconsider the dependency.

M0 — Prepare and pin

Inspect actual worktree, build instructions, existing terrain API, scheduler, saves, branch status and unpublished changes. Create isolated reviewed implementation branch/worktree from appropriate code head and import canonical Brain decisions; don’t base runtime work on docs alone without checking differences. Freeze dependency manifest, source/license notices, units/clock, world seed and scenario/tolerance definitions. Runtime scope is authorized by the user on 26 November 2026; synchronize the active task and instructions into the actual implementation checkout. Don’t delete legacy data or overwrite another writer. Deliver reproducible build and start fresh-world policy.

M1 — One aged editable world

Integrate GeoGenesis core, selected PWG geometry/morphology and existing Zylann. Form macro geography, age at chosen years, export final original bed/material/climate/drainage (not filled diagnostic water stock), build feature-first channel/basin geometry, initialize finite stock later. Generate visible small valley with 1m tributary, curved river, lake, closed depression, mountains and editable land; test adjacent seams and matching hydraulic curves from same solids. Use real authored/generated geometry, not fixture-only solver. Deliver visible terrain, world age/density/shape configuration, finite geometry summaries and macro 4,000km count/bytes/startup census without full fine generation. If fine tributaries do not respect parent outlet, fix recipe before adding water.

M2 — Water replacement (highest priority)

Integrate upstream Wflow numerical routines, connected independently staged subgrid compartments, finite volume exchange; CFE native soil/runoff, finite Wflow-derived groundwater/springs and coastal/ocean inventory. Ordinary reach+flood storage first; use FluxCell only for needed rapid detailed patches and demonstrate conservative authority transfer and actual geometry/withdrawal updates. Do not port full Julia stack or rebuild old graph. Render real stage-clipped water, query depth/current cheaply, include dams/weirs and narrow channel controls. Meter rainfall as a temporary explicit forcing fixture, not production weather. Deliver playable filling, draining, extraction, rainfall/runoff, backwater, protected settlement, gate opening/reversal, wet excavation, caves and save/reload with offscreen progression. No double-stock, infinite source, ghost water across sealed barrier or widespread fine-grid requirement. If geometry/positivity/stability work turns Wflow into a new general-purpose solver, stop and reconsider regional dependency; do not hide it in adapters. If rapid-flow area exceeds FluxCell budget, record missing regional capability, don’t clamp away physically consequential floods.

M3 — Live coupled weather

Implement Weather3D reduced numerical core on CPU-compatible packed fields using established pressure backend, conservative MC/TVD fluxes, selected CliMA bulk phase and finite falling rain/snow, COARE-informed exchange and surface heat capacity. Generate actual wind/rain responses to topography, temperature, humidity, seasons and water; connect CFE/surface/GW, remove temporary rain driver from normal path. Cloud physics snapshot supplies base/top, condensate, types and movement. Require mass and simplified energy accounting of all physical exchanges and explicit boundary/solar energy, stable circulation with meaningful wet/dry and mountain rain-shadow behavior, no arbitrary rainfall source or doubled ET. Drought and rewetting must affect both sides. Provide field queries for gameplay independent of renderer. If the native reduced dynamics port becomes a rewrite or cannot maintain stability/physical finite coupling without large compute, return the specific blocker instead of adding a heavyweight atmosphere.

M4 — Erosion and visual world

Reuse GeoGenesis bed/hillslope/material methods where applicable and MeanderPy slow alluvial migration/cutoffs, driven by actual accumulated flow/shear, sediment, wind, rainfall and material/vegetation resistance; protected hard/engineered banks constrain movement. Separate fast stage/wetted width from slow physical bed/centerline changes. Apply bounded persistent solid edits and repair water/navigation; cutoffs retain finite stock and connectivity, not cosmetic curves. Integrate SunshineClouds2 real volumetric compositor: start quarter-linear resolution 480x270 at 1080p, 24–48 primary samples, low lighting sample count, early-out/empty-space skipping and depth-aware temporal reconstruction. Default stock settings not accepted. Shape driven by persistent meteorological formations, reuse 3D density shapes where supported, dynamic sunlight, beautiful open-sky gaps when weather permits. Do not build second weather simulator or fixed baked sun shading. Deliver attractive normal gameplay view below/above/inside clouds, moving sun/camera, storm, clean curved streams and changing water stage/terrain. Keep measured frame/VRAM costs, not screenshot-only pass.

M5 — Complete release evidence and scale

Use same environment implementation to run normal connected valley and 100x100km severe storm/recession, representative 256km region, and 4,000km macro with multiple simultaneous active regions and sparse terrain/solver residency. Do not equate idle macro load with a fully active world. Include offscreen edits, dams, dry/wet, finite snow/ice and heat, cave/ocean/groundwater exchange, representative gameplay depth/hazard/nav queries, paced save/reload and actual rendered host. Report accepted finite residuals, water levels/arrival/hazard/extent against simple analytical or existing fine reference, no false closed-barrier crossing. Choose gameplay-relevant error thresholds before work; historical 5cm/10%/IoU0.9 are proposed fixture starting tolerances not universal scientific accuracy or new accepted requirements. Record exact source/binary/config and inclusive CPU/GPU/RAM/debt/p95/p99/frame/latency, generation/age separately, actual visible captures. Prove no global scan or full fine promotion. Record actual success/failure and unresolved features; only promote validated capabilities and leave future scale/ECS/ecology/other-liquid work open. Prefer a smaller truthful functioning release with named open failures to falsely labeling 4,000km/8x production-ready.

7. Stop conditions and reporting

No competing full solver builds, blanket dependency upgrades, old water optimization, unapproved license, invented source implementation, or numerical redesign masquerading as integration. Preserve tests as ordinary development, not months of independent qualification. When a hard requirement fails, show exact source/fixture/profile, why reuse cannot solve it cheaply, and the smallest viable replacement/contract change; obtain approval if changing fundamental gameplay, clocks or dependency/license. Do not hide unresolved full hydraulic, pressure, rendering or population costs. No separate repeated paper search unless a concrete blocker. Commit bounded code+evidence on reviewable branch, update canonical Brain systems, candidate dispositions and traceability. Never edit generated website repo; run atlas generation/check/tests and publish through configured workflow. This plan makes actual technology choices provisional, not a claim of new performance or integration validation.

8. User-approved full replacement execution

The user approved the implementation-first design and all M1–M5 delivery work on 26 November 2026. The active work is full replacement, not an optional branch of the old environment system. Follow the execution protocol for orchestration, continuous progress and public Brain publication. No milestone requires another user permission unless it exposes a fundamental decision or real blocker. M0 is preparation, not a further research gate. Minimal targeted verification is required for the changed path only; do not reproduce comprehensive legacy compatibility or vendor validation. Preserve truthful status, hard physical invariants, representative scale evidence and repository safety. Remove or disconnect obsolete environmental paths where the replacement is ready, without deleting unrelated assets, saves or unpublished changes. Proposed tunable settings remain configurable; routine reversible engineering decisions are delegated. No measured performance or runtime implementation is established by this authorization.
