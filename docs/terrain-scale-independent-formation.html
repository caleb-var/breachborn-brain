Scale-independent terrain formation — research and proposed delivery

28 November 2026. User-approved problem/goal: increasing the world extent must add geography, not enlarge local features or reduce physical or visible detail per kilometre. The current cloud/precipitation worker continues independently. Address terrain, water and clouds before a vegetation or broad lighting/art-polish campaign. This is a researched implementation recommendation, not measured new capability or authorization to replace selected solvers. Read visual requirements, current terrain audit, active V1 plan, LOD contract and ecology system.

Exact implementation audit

Runtime b1b7f382, visual-foundation branch. The 4,000 km accepted live replay is an old 250²/16 km recipe, not a new schema-6 geographic qualification. Schema-6 tests cover one 64 × 48 km/1 km world. The matched cliff 96/48 m vs 64/32 m LOD comparison raised data blocks from 20,146 to 24,018 and one probe to LOD0 without meaningful image improvement. Keep this evidence; solve missing geography before increasing mesh residency.

  • Pinned MIT GeoGenesis initial pipeline chooses base relief cycles from extent/800 km clamped to 3–9, so base continental structure is not a substitute for constant-metre local relief. Stamp placement targets round(density × 16), cap 40, regardless of world area; stamp length parameter 8–34 cells and width parameter 2.5–7.5 cells. At 1 km, length parameter is 8–34 km; at 16 km it is 128–544 km. This is parameter size, not a guarantee of actual feature dimensions. The default 1.25 regional stamp density requests ~20 candidates, subject to placement. Whole-world tectonic plates may properly have continent-scale support; regional features need separate metric frequency and eligibility.
  • Regional landforms discard baked wavelengths below four cells, gate gorges/glacial corridors at <=4 km and karst at <=1.5 km. Sampler bilinearly interpolates macro bed and adds slope/anchor/shore-constrained 32–256 m noise and schema-6 640–2,048 m maximum wavelength extra relief, with a 32 m combined parameter ceiling. Protected cells suppress it. This is not a regional valley network.
  • Hydrography builds reach geometry using D8 source cell/receiver corridors, spacing-dependent search/carve constraints, and IDs based on cell index. 20 channel-initiation cells means 20 km² at 1 km or 5,120 km² at 16 km; the documented 8 km fixture uses 80 cells to retain the macro threshold. A coarse trunk graph must not be mistaken for all local drainage. Metric thresholds by drainage role and actual runoff/terrain are required.
  • Recipe exporter runs GeoGenesis time evolution on one lattice and applies regional features before final climate/route export. It admits <=1,024 cells/axis and >=1 km spacing. Existing cell record is 76 bytes (decoder); full state costs much more than a height raster. Current world readers and IDs assume one flat cell-indexed formation. Changing that contract needs a versioned, reviewed refinement boundary, not simply a larger array.
  • LandWater::from_catchments maps formation cells to finite catchment patches by outlet and aggregates area, soil and temperature. WeatherWater::prepare_world_footprints already sets a separate capped atmosphere grid and overlaps formation footprints. SurfaceWater and hydrography still reference the formation lattice directly. Retain the macro solver grid and existing owners initially; define regional geometric summaries and aggregation rather than creating a runtime owner per refined sample or forcing atmospheric columns finer.

Recommended architecture: one canonical formation, multiple physical scales

Use a versioned immutable formation recipe with stable world-metre coordinates, geological history, seeded feature identity and a single deterministic terrain query. Distinguish geographic detail, storage pages, physical simulation granularity, and visual LOD. Do not generate each chunk as an independent world, rescale all feature bands with total dimensions, use independent camera-dependent erosion, or create a second authoritative heightmap.

Candidate scale budget over 4,000 km square: 16 km 62,500 samples; 8 km 250,000; 4 km 1M; 1 km 16M; 250 m 256M; 125 m 1.024B. At 4 bytes/sample a full height-only raster costs 0.25 MB, 1 MB, 4 MB, 64 MB, 1.024 GB, 4.096 GB respectively (decimal, no mips or other fields). Current 76-byte formation records would instead be 4.75 MB, 19 MB, 76 MB, 1.216 GB, 19.456 GB, 77.824 GB before other costs; these latter figures are illustrative, not proposed formats. A 32 km/125 m page is 256² = 65,536 samples, ~256 KiB of raw float height before halo/fields. Whole-world fine geometry storage may be feasible only at selected scales and compression; full resident time evolution is not implied. Measure actual birth, build, load, disk, cache, worker CPU/GPU and peak RAM/VRAM. Compare cheap precomputed regional summaries or streamed bake against deterministic lazy materialization. Do not impose a universal fine raster or arbitrary memory quota without evidence.

Metric provinces and feature registry

Separate absolute world width/depth, formation spacing and feature spectrum. Generate tectonic/climatic provinces at appropriate large scale, then regional feature candidates from deterministic metric regions and eligible area/density (per km²), geology, precipitation, relief, age, slope and river discharge. Use global stable coordinates and versioned hash/seed domain keys, deterministic ownership of overlaps and shared borders; seeded clustering/spacing by type, not fixed quotas per world or uniform terrain stamps. Feature count scales with eligible area, not a naive multiply of plate count, and different climates have legitimately different counts. Feature profiles have widths/lengths/heights in metres (peaks/ridges, canyon/gorge, cliff, karst, terrace, coastal, glacial where supported) and live physical material/soil. Keep mountain belts and regional lowlands meaningful; do not raise global vertical scale indiscriminately. Introduce a small explicit feature catalogue and use existing PWG morphology kernels, including unresolved narrow formations at regional/local scale. New generation settings include independent extent, coarse spacing, feature density/size ranges, regional target spacing, age and stage; choose configurable defaults from evidence. Do not require exact same absolute geography under changing world extent (global boundary/climate can change); require deterministic same recipe and comparable local detail distributions at matched conditions.

Hydrology-first regional refinement

  1. Run GeoGenesis once on the global coarse grid using metric controls. Keep actual aged bed separate from filled routing surface; preserve trunk channels, basin terminals, spill elevations, catchment boundaries, sea and climate/geology. Record coarse anchor and stable feature/port identities. Do not regenerate a separate GeoGenesis world per page.
  2. Refine a representative watershed region from that parent, using metric PWG directional landform kernels and conditioned base elevation/gradient. Include neighboring halo, consistent ridge divides and shared port positions/stages; use constrained interpolation, not just noise. Add drainage-aware ridges/valleys/tributaries and eligible landforms. Route runoff using actual fine bed, known upstream contributions and downstream conditions. Reuse existing implicit stream-power/hillslope and bounded generation-time detail where valuable; keep outlet profiles and true depressions. A local algorithm may be allowed to discover a changed divide/spill but must reconcile affected parent topology before publication rather than silently forcing contradictory routing or ignoring the change.
  3. Use the existing spatial region directory/cache and one versioned refinement page format (or existing equivalent) with read-only halo, non-overlapping core custody and deterministic same-metre samples. Shared edge records and globally keyed procedural features; independent page requests/threads/order must give identical results. A halo handles local stencils, not global drainage. For long-range effects solve parent/edge spill and contribution graph, reconcile cross-page dependencies, then publish immutable page + summary atomically. Boundary port includes stable reach/basin ID, bed/profile, stage/flow direction, contributing area and connectivity; validate downstream/terminal and parent conservation. Store only needed generated fields or reproducible residuals, not all intermediate erosion history. Precompute enough fine-geography summaries at birth to establish offscreen land/water topology; if full fine page creation is deferred, pure deterministic reconstruction cannot retroactively alter an accepted parent network, water inventory, prior rain, erosion or player edits. If it can, precompute/reconcile the dependent geometry before accepting that owner. Camera requests only residency. No placeholder physical water or sealed crossings hidden by culling.
  4. Existing water owners use aggregated catchments, reaches and basins; new local channels use the same current terrain and hydraulic graph. Metric stream initiation thresholds by tier, contributing area and discharge (not one raw cell count), allow small streams via aggregate runoff when individual explicit channels are not warranted. Use existing width/depth/cross-section and finite custody, restrict/promote local stores and flows conservatively, update only affected revision/receivers when terrain edits. Do not create one permanent dynamic owner for every fine height sample; require mass/heat/sediment conservation and equal downstream consequence before/after refinement. Preserve lateral caves, coast receiver ownership, flood support, evaporation/precipitation overlaps and existing save/reopen evidence. Regional elevation, slope, coastal fractions and wet area can be summarized for existing atmospheric and land footprints. Atmosphere grid cadence/resolution does not follow terrain mesh or refinement spacing. A new immutable formation requires new dependent owners/checkpoint once; don’t use old physical state with different bed.
  5. Runtime sample = immutable parent + versioned deterministic regional/local geometry + accepted edit overlay, all in metre coordinates. Refine visual meshes and material maps independently; far geometry is a filtered sample of the same physical world. Current SDF/edits own solid truth and local revisions invalidate only relevant summaries/mesh/water supports. Preserve origin rebasing. Water visual contours and flow use current support, coherent shared material/shoreline detail across LOD, no cosmetic flooding of dry ground. Cloud worker continues the approved shapes and precipitation path; after integration sample revised terrain and accepted weather, no duplicate weather solver or restarts. Vegetation/grass and broader lighting/art effects are deferred until the three environment systems satisfy this foundation.

Acceptance gates

  • Extent scaling: distribution of landform widths/heights, local relief/slope, stream density/order and visual detail within comparable terrain classes does not collapse as dimensions grow; large-world regions contain comparable detail in matched kilometre windows. No requirement every place is rugged or identical; validate multiple geographic regimes and raw physical values, not screenshot-only. Establish realistic/artist-approved ranges from small reference crops; avoid invented universal numerical thresholds.
  • Topology: shared edge samples match exactly for same canonical coordinate, no seams or request-order dependence, valid downstream graph and basin/sea contacts, known contributing area and finite ownership once, no unphysical new river/catchment due only to viewer. Local edits affect current support and downstream records; save/reopen reproduce accepted state.
  • Efficiency: bound resident fine pages and voxel meshes by workload; no dense metre terrain or solver cell per visual sample, no repeated world births, finite generation/loading memory and measured frame pacing. Existing 8x/60 FPS target, no claims from terrain-only or old-world replays.
  • Visual: true 4,000 km overview plus equal-scale regional/local views with distinct mountainous and lowland regions, natural drainage/coast, interesting intermediate landforms and continuity during zoom. Cloud/weather and water remain coupled and attractive. User visual review.
  • Do not claim these gates are passed by this document. No new runtime or benchmark performed during planning.

Controlling specification — user direction, 28 November 2026

Reuse existing GeoGenesis, PWG ports, native metric feature/refinement code, existing hydraulic and land/air owners, Zylann and Godot. No new open-source dependency, terrain engine, erosion solver or renderer is authorized. Earlier research comparisons are context only. Explore alternatives only for a demonstrated missing capability that existing code cannot reasonably supply, with explicit approval before integration.

Generation

  • World extent in metres determines amount of geography, never local feature size, erosion/detail wavelength, density per eligible area, channel initiation area or material scale. A 1 km feature stays 1 km. Preserve equivalent local/regional richness in matched physical windows across small and large worlds, allowing meaningful differences in climate, tectonics and geology. Same recipe deterministic; identical geography across different world extents not required. Larger worlds may additionally have larger continental structures.
  • Distinct mountain chains, individual ridges/peaks/spurs, branching valleys, hills, escarpments, gorges/ravines, plateaus, varied coasts, real river/lake/floodplain/tributary geometry, and conditionally appropriate karst, volcanic, arid and glacial forms through the existing selected kernels. Preserve real plains; no uniform noise or indiscriminate height increase.
  • GeoGenesis step already schedules uplift, fluvial, hillslope, glacial, wind, coast and chemical processes; fluvial supports implicit incision and deposition, hillslope diffusion/soil production/landslides. The exporter runs actual aging before regional post-passes. Coarse aging cannot resolve fine mountain valleys, and post-generated relief has not automatically undergone that age history. Reuse current erosion/landform kernels at meaningful bounded physical scales, with parent age, climate, geology, slopes and drainage as inputs. Where needed use bounded finer geological erosion before publishing final geometry; age-conditioned approximations are permissible if coherent and labeled. No globally dense fine aging or new erosion solver. Preserve real runoff, material/soil/sediment and drainage consequences and genuine aging playback.

Data

Start with the existing sparse representation: immutable aged coarse anchors; compact world-metre metric feature records and spatial buckets (existing 28-byte records); optional bounded refined samples/cache only when needed for erosion, routing or query efficiency; connected catchment/reach/basin/spill/receiver records; existing persistent voxel edit overlay; derived filtered meshes/materials. Reuse the existing region directory rather than mandate a new tile framework. Earlier proposed spacings are research candidates, not fixed dense raster or solver grids. Page size must not set feature size or create seams. Keep deterministic geometry, stable IDs, exact shared edges, single current solid authority and revisioned local edits. Weather/land/water solver scales remain independent. Compute enough regional connectivity/ownership summaries before admitting physical state; camera only requests residency. Reconcile actual changed divides, sinks, outlets, coast and water support, conserve finite stocks, aggregate smaller runoff where explicit owners unnecessary. No per-height-sample physical solver.

Worker 6791438d reports schema-8 single 2 km core/1 km halo runoff pilot with one dry-land parent reach and cold continuation. Retain it, generalize connected support beyond the one site; its refusal of new sinks, other outlets, coasts and caves, zero residual outside the core and time/save hold on intersecting edits are pilot guards, not acceptable permanent geography restrictions. No claim of general or continent-scale qualification.

Independent implementation and sequence review — 28 November 2026

Review of the original V1 implementation-first plan, user update, integrated e9c4a268, fresh worker b9a8f364 and the steep port review. This is planning advice, not approval to introduce a new physical owner, a benchmark or actual visual acceptance. What has been learned

The sequence of schemas 7–14, connected metric cores, narrower aperture, standalone aged watershed, bounded 250 m erosion and ridge hierarchy established useful edit/refinement, exact edges, finite runoff and independent continuation boundaries. The measured ridge addition changed local crests 111–149 m and skyline about 23 pixels but 64 km p90 relief only 688.51→689.60 m; bounded finer erosion altered 337 nodes, median 7.87 m among those, and 141 internal routes with similar skyline. These are useful physical/geometry checks, not major landscape improvements. Repeatedly preserving existing receivers, closed lows, channel beds, basin/coast support and stocks constrains fresh morphology unnecessarily. Retain these checks for changing existing live worlds; use fresh formation topology derived from the finished physical bed before creating any finite owners. No further archived shoulder-cut/location-search campaign as a prerequisite to new terrain.

Fresh schema 15 implementation and evidence is on its worker branch at review time, not yet the integrated head. It produced 81 ridges, 31 spurs, 13 valley axes on 100×100 km/1 km anchors, completed four actual 10,000-year age steps with zero repairs, 2,789 m max bed, aggregate 10,000 km² area and runoff audit, a 600→660 s continuation. It reports 739 vs 178 m p90 two-km relief across rugged/lower adjacent windows, 850 m p90 in 64 km and 65.94 km represented channels. Wide terrain and shallow/sloping profile issues remain. Its held views are not an advancing frame-rate qualification.

Specific scale blockers: fresh_geography.rs samples ridge cross relief at ±1,900 m and the spur shoulders at ~1,120–1,380 m using bilinear parent heights; crest screening rejects drainage >18 km² and spur screening requires <12 km². With 4 km anchors every D8 cell starts at 16 km², so the latter condition cannot pass, and the code rejects formations lacking any of the three feature roles. Do not fix by multiplying all thresholds or lengths with cell size, or requiring mountains in all climates. Evaluate eligibility against resolved metric contributing area and geological support, not the coarsened lattice; make missing legitimate classes a reported observation rather than a universal generation failure. At 4 km, observed median full spur and valley widths 2.06/2.65 km are below one interval. Decoder/sampler deliberately treats fresh metric records as provenance, skips metric indexing/slopes and disables previous local/metric refinement; the actual bed between anchors is bilinear plus ordinary channel carve. This is correct protection against reapplying unaged stamps, but it loses sub-anchor shape. Metric shape is a smooth tapered elliptical kernel; adding such positive relief alone tends toward rounded shoulders (hypothesis to test). Fresh schedule applies metric geometry before one final geological interval on the same coarse grid; it does not age sub-anchor valleys. Use existing sparse records/aged residual and bounded native erosion/route kernels to carry resolved aged shapes, not blindly add the original stamps after aging. At fresh birth, allow changed drainage/basins and rebuild affected geology/climate, route, surface and finite owners; preserve true depressions and reconcile real outlet/sill geometry.

The hydrography source builds a channel per D8 link, rejects raw bed rise and crossed diagonals, uses linear endpoint bank grade and limited lateral corridor; steep reaches can be straight. Filled routing area closure is not a certificate of represented channel connectivity. Fresh selected valley pair lacked a shared receiver; another pair reported 1.140 m³/s with 0.161 m projected stage gap and zero next outlet, local stream 0.0997 m depth. Separate visually legible wet corridor, real upstream/downstream reach/terminal, and meaningful live physical transport from diagnostic point results. Do not force a meander on steep rock, excavate a raw rise blindly or paint missing water; establish a consistent graded valley/river or real basin spill before owner creation. Need one representative river with actual adequate water and wet support, not every reach solved in a small formation.
