First terrain integration, 26 November 2026

Implementation commit 1b83f78f. Built the unchanged GeoGenesis MIT core aa898282cbdd with pinned Rust 1.82.0 and called its formation, age and final geology/climate/D8 routing functions. A versioned immutable native archive supplies the original bed to the Zylann generator and retains geological and climate fields for downstream consumers. It carries depression-filled elevation and annual discharge as diagnostics. Zylann retains current voxels and applies edits.

The fixed parcel is 64×48 km with 1 km macro samples and seed 713. Four 10,000-year steps changed all 3,072 bed cells by more than 0.01 m with zero reported numerical repairs. Two exports produced the same 234,808-byte archive. Birth, age and final refresh took 9.94 and 10.17 ms together. Including serialization and file synchronization, exports took 67.39 and 75.79 ms. Thirty-three cells had more than 0.01 m of potential depression fill. The corrected native reader accepted the upstream three-class river mask.

Display-backed Godot 4.5.1 and the pinned patched Zylann extension ran on an RTX 2070 SUPER with the OpenGL renderer. In the checked cell, original bed was 634.399 m and filled diagnostic elevation was 677.239 m. LOD0, LOD2 and the streamed current voxel agreed within the preset 0.02 m SDF tolerance. Editing the voxel changed its SDF from −2.396 to +1.999 m while the immutable source remained −2.399 m. The 452 ms local streaming interval reported 3,935 generator calls and 326 mesh blocks. The captured frame showed a plain coarse surface with visible creases. The guarded build and display run completed in 19.78 s and 5.83 s respectively.

Verification covers this parcel and an in-session edit in the terrain inspection scene. Geological textures and fine geometry, PWG river realization, cold save continuation, default gameplay adoption and live scale remain open.
