Environmental numerical probes and revised efficiency shortlist

25 November 2026. The user was not satisfied with source-only technology selection and requested concrete calculations and true attractive volumetric clouds. These independently authored probes were executed in a Linux virtualized AMD EPYC 9V74 environment, not the target Ryzen 7 3700X / RTX 2070 SUPER. Independent dam-break experiment

Frictionless one-dimensional 1,000 m channel, unit width, reservoir depth 1 m left of x=0, dry right, reflective boundaries beyond wave reach, g=9.81 m/s2; t=20 physical s. Local-inertial test omits advective momentum, uses max adjacent depth for staggered face depth and donor-limited explicit flux; HLL finite-volume comparison evolves depth and momentum with dry-bed wave-speed estimates. CFL coefficient 0.35. Neither is a copy or execution of Wflow/SFINCS/FluxCell/legacy code. Ritter exact depth in fan: (2sqrt(g h0)-x/t)^2/(9g). At 1 cm depth, analytic front 106.4911 m. Report cell-center threshold front, and L1 depth integral divided by h0 sqrt(g h0) t; not a standard relative domain RMSE.

Conservation is necessary, not adequate accuracy. Local-inertia failure here is expected for an extreme dry-bed dam break, not proof against low-Froude use or all production implementations. de Almeida and Bates, 2013 reports slower unsteady wave propagation and generally good agreement at Fr below 0.5 in tested flows. Not evidence for a universal 1 km mesh.

At hypothetical combined horizontal wind 30 m/s, C=0.5, dx=16 km, step 266.67 physical s; applying illustrative historical alpha=144 physical seconds per game second yields 1.49 CPU-ms/game-second for four-scalar AVX2 transport only. At 8 km this becomes 10.73 ms. Alpha is not automatically an accepted atmosphere clock; vertical motion/pressure may force shorter steps. A broad field can be cheap and accurate at 16 km, small storms cannot be justified at that grid from this evidence. Conservative semi-Lagrangian split remapping (UK Met Office SLICE) is an alternative to investigate if explicit stability/dispersion limits dominate. Do not select Weather3D wholesale; historical source review found global mass rescaling and nonfinite evaporation. Moist shallow-water models are a useful reduced-dynamics reference, not independently qualified circulation. Benchmark diagnostic terrain/thermal wind versus reduced prognostic circulation with real evaporation, cloud/rain and heat feedback.

Detailed water and geometry

FluxCell pin c62d8118839831529399b33815bac52a94738f55, MIT code (license; data separately licensed), API, verification. Preprint 24 Nov 2026 reports 610–700M cell updates/s on an integrated laptop GPU for fused second-order MUSCL-Hancock/HLLC. Upstream author measurement, not independently reproduced, preprint not peer reviewed. CPU reference available; mutable bed keeps water depth, solid mask fixed, batch readbacks and ownership require qualification. Do not import full global grid or confuse CFL update throughput with simulated time. Clawpack BSD Riemann kernels/reference remain alternatives. mizuRoute IRF/Lagrangian kinematic/diffusive methods (documentation) motivate cheap ordinary downstream routing, not universal backwater or mutable topology. Verify license and source pin before code reuse. Prior Wflow/SFINCS references remain valid for low-Froude subgrid geometry; no preferred old native flux implementation.

At historical alpha=144, hypothetical 100x100 km region, h=2 m, gravity-wave C=0.5 and two faces/cell: 250 m requires ~1.63M face updates/game-s, 1 km ~25.5k (64x less). An illustrative 8 CPU-ms/game-s surface budget allows ~4.9 vs 314 ns/face; actual stability, graph structure and memory change this. Scaling historical reach density 102,321/512^2 km2 across 4,000^2 km2 gives ~6.245M reaches, ~381 MiB at 64B/reach before links/curves, not a required density. Use catchment-scale summaries with finite water and geometry-consistent narrow streams, admit consequential details independent of camera. Do not generate all fine terrain to derive geometry. Geometry/accuracy remains an unresolved risk.

True volumetric clouds

SunshineClouds2, MIT, source-inspected Godot compositor. Supports 3D noise textures, painted mask, depth-aware reduced resolution, temporal reprojection, point effectors, directional lights; current Godot project advertises 4.5 Forward+. Its defaults 300 ray steps, 32 lighting max, half resolution; nested light loops and point-effector loop in density sampling make stock configuration unsuitable to assume cheap. Global floor/ceiling and noise/mask structure do not establish independently shaped vertical formations. Godot-Cloud-Worlds, MIT, source-inspected pre-baked SDF skipping and single light-march per pixel, but stochastic density mask and transparency/shadow limitations. Clayjohn sky demo v2 spreads hemisphere updates over 64 frames; useful distant sky concept, not local fly-through solution. Guerrilla Nubis Evolved reports under 2 ms PS4 sky clouds and Nubis Cubed describes voxel density, SDF skipping and cached lighting; commercial engine performance not transferable.

Preferred first render comparison: real bounded 3D density raymarch with pre-generated reusable shaped volumes, projected tile bounds/scene depth, quarter linear resolution (480x270 at 1080p), 24–48 primary steps, cheap single scattering/precomputed approximate optical depth or 1–2 shadow samples, early termination, depth-aware temporal reconstruction and history rejection on motion. Compare configured Sunshine addon to narrow bounded density renderer and pre-baked SDF approach. True volumetric near, distant sky representations optional. No expensive simulation or random noise as shape authority. At 32 primary steps quarter resolution all-screen ceiling 4.147M samples vs full HD 64 steps 132.71M, 32x fewer; 25% projected covered rays 1.037M if actually culled, not guaranteed. Eight 128^3 R8 volumes consume 16 MiB excluding mipmaps and cache/light data. No GPU timing or art quality validation performed. Proposed normal cloud target <=1 ms and adverse <=2 ms at reference 1080p only for experiment; not approved budget. Measure below/inside/above, sunrise/night, overcast, storm, fast time, camera movement, scene-depth compositing and shadows. Do not adopt a second heavyweight weather simulator for rendering.
